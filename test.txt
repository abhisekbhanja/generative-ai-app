Employee Handbook - TechNova Solutions (Sample Knowledge Base for RAG Testing)
This document is fictional and is intended for testing Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) applications. It contains information across multiple topics so that semantic search and question answering can be evaluated.
Company Overview
TechNova Solutions is a software company founded in 2018. The headquarters is located in Bengaluru, India.
The company develops cloud-native applications, AI solutions, and enterprise platforms.
The CEO is Ananya Rao and the CTO is Vikram Mehta.
Working Hours
Employees work Monday through Friday.
Standard office hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM.
Hybrid employees are required to work from the office at least three days each week.
Leave Policy
Employees receive 20 paid leave days per year.
Casual leave is limited to 8 days.
Sick leave is limited to 10 days.
Unused casual leave cannot be carried forward.
Maternity leave is 26 weeks.
Paternity leave is 15 days.
Travel Policy
Flights under 800 km should be booked in Economy class.
Hotel reimbursement is limited to ₹6,000 per night unless pre-approved.
Taxi expenses require digital receipts.
IT Support
Resetting passwords requires MFA verification.
The IT helpdesk email is helpdesk@technova.example.
Critical incidents have a response time of 30 minutes.
Normal priority requests are resolved within two business days.
Technology Stack
Backend services use Java 21 and Spring Boot.
Frontend applications use React.
Microservices communicate using REST and Kafka.
Kubernetes is used for deployment.
NGINX Ingress handles external traffic.
PostgreSQL is the primary database.
Redis is used for caching.
Security Policy
Passwords must contain at least 12 characters.
Multi-factor authentication is mandatory.
USB storage devices are blocked by default.
Confidential data must be encrypted at rest and in transit.
Training
New employees complete onboarding within five days.
Annual cybersecurity training is mandatory.
Cloud certification costs are reimbursed after successful completion.
